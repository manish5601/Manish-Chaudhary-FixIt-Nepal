# FixIt Nepal: User Guide 📖

Welcome to **FixIt Nepal**! This guide will walk you through the core features of our platform based on your user role.

---

## 👤 For Customers

### 1. Registration & Profile
- **Sign Up**: Click **Register** and select **Customer**. Fill in your details and upload a profile picture.
- **Login**: Use your email and password to access your dashboard.

### 2. Finding a Service
- **Search**: Use the search bar on the home page or browse categories.
- **Maps**: Click **Find Nearby** to see a map of available service providers in your area.
- **Requests**: If you have a custom task, post a **Service Request** and wait for providers to bid.

### 3. Booking a Service
- **Select Slot**: Choose a provider and click **Book Now**. Select an available date and time slot.
- **Payment**: To confirm, pay the Rs. 10 token amount via **eSewa**. 
- **Confirmation**: Once the token is paid, the provider will be notified.

### 4. Communication & Completion
- **Chat**: Use the **Chat** icon in your booking details to talk to the provider.
- **Reviews**: After the service is completed, leave a rating and comment to help others.

---

## 🛠️ For Service Providers

### 1. Registration & Verification 
- **Sign Up**: Register as a **Service Provider**. Select your primary category and upload your **Citizenship/Document**.
- **Verification**: An admin will review your documents. You cannot receive bookings until you are **Approved**.

### 2. Managing Availability
- **Schedule**: Go to **Profile > Manage Availability**. 
- **Time Slots**: Set your working hours and off-days so customers can book valid slots.

### 3. Handling Bookings
- **Requests**: You will receive notifications for new bookings. 
- **Action**: You have **24 hours** to **Accept** or **Reject** a booking after the token is paid.
- **Navigation**: Use the provided Google Maps link to find the customer's location.

---

## 🛡️ For Admins

### 1. Verification Dashboard
- **Review**: Go to **Admin > Manage Providers**. 
- **Approve**: Open a provider's pending application, inspect the uploaded documents, and click **Verify**.

### 2. Moderation
- **Reviews**: If a review is flagged as inappropriate, you can **Delete** it or **Dismiss the Flag**.
- **Disputes**: View open disputes between customers and providers. Communicate with both parties and mark the dispute as **Resolved**.

### 3. Platform Oversight
- **Analytics**: Monitor the number of active bookings and current marketplace health.
